## 2. What the live site actually did (the gap)

Only **one of four** was published as written. The other three were changed by the old process — which
is exactly what the call said should stop.

- **#1 (Yik Jinn) — rewritten.** Email headline "Before You Hire From an AI Shortlist, Check Who
  Published Its Sources" became "AI Search Tools May Rely on Marketing Agencies' Own Websites…". The
  subheads changed too (e.g. "Most of the Links Lead Back to the Sellers" → "Most AI Citations Lead to
  Marketing Providers' Websites"). The byline "By Yik Jinn…" was kept.
- **#3 (Reto Sahli) — re-titled.** Email "What If Malaysia's Greatest Competitive Advantage Isn't Cost?"
  became "Malaysia's Competitive Advantage Goes Beyond Cost, Says HAVI Digital Leader". Byline kept.
- **#4 (industrial talent) — re-titled.** Email "What the next generation of Southeast Asian industrial
  talent needs to learn" became "What Southeast Asia's Next Generation of Industrial Talent Needs to
  Learn". No byline in the article or the page.
- **#2 (Workplace) — as-is.** Headline, standfirst, "By Satveer Bhatt…", body and subheads match the
  article. **This is the target look.**

Also worth showing: #1 and #4 have **no author photo**, yet they are live — so the "is the author photo
required?" question is real, not hypothetical.

## 3. Questions for MSME

### A. Must answer before we build the behaviour (blocking)

1. **As-is or rewrite?** The call said publish contributed pieces as they are, but the site re-titled/
   reworded 3 of the 4. Going forward: publish **word-for-word** (headline, subheads, body unchanged),
   or keep re-titling/rewording? → *Show #1 and #3 (email vs live page).*
2. **Category:** use **insights** (the spec), or the site's topic categories such as "Career &
   Professional Development"? → *Show #2's live page.*
3. **Author photo:** is it **required before publishing** (hold until it arrives), or do we publish
   without it? → *Show #1 and #4, which are live with no author photo.*
4. **When the article has no byline** (#4): do we **ask the sender who the author is**, or credit the
   sender/publisher? → *Show #4.*

### B. Defaults we will build with unless told otherwise

5. **Trigger words:** treat "contribute", "Contributed Article", "[Bylined Article]" and "Research pitch"
   as the markers (all four examples). Any others?
6. **Author source:** document "By …" line first, then an "About the author" note, then ask the sender;
   never use the agency when a document byline exists.
7. **Byline format:** "By <Name>, <Role>" (as the site shows).
8. **Headline:** keep the article's own headline exactly (no editor re-title) — unless answer 1 says
   re-titling is allowed.
9. **Dateline + standfirst:** add the email date as "DATE – <the article's own standfirst>", as the site
   does on #2.
10. **Subheads:** keep the article's own subheadings on the page.
11. **What we may change:** only remove web links / emails / phone numbers and move the "By …" and
    "About the author" lines out of the body (the page validator forbids contacts in the body). Nothing
    else changes.
12. **Editorial filter:** the normal 3R/score filter still runs on contributed pieces; only the
    repurposing (rewrite) step is skipped.
13. **Timing:** ask for the author photo first; run the filter and publish after it arrives.
14. **The photo ask:** a headshot, JPG/PNG, at least 800 px wide — is that the sentence you want to send?
15. **Where the photo shows:** as the article's banner / featured image only, captioned "<Name>, <Role>"?
16. **Replies:** one reply per article, as today?
17. **Scope:** contributed/byline pieces only, or also opinion/commentary pieces?

### C. Our engineering calls (no MSME answer needed)

18. Guard against a normal press release that merely contains the word "contribute" in its body — it
    stays a press release.
19. Store the author and role as two extra fields on the checklist row (the editor and export read them).
20. Build a **no-AI verbatim draft**: the article's own title, standfirst, byline, paragraphs and
    subheads, laid out in the engine's required shape, with category insights.
21. Keep the article **held** (not sent to the engine) while the author or photo is missing, so it is
    never paraphrased.
22. The verbatim draft must still pass the engine's page checks (no contact details in the body, a
    featured image present).
