# D-004 — House style v2 (news format + commentary branch)

## Rule

Where the docx and live practice disagree, the pipeline follows **measured live practice**:

1. **News format** — subheaders 3–8 (median 5; docx says 3–4); excerpt 25–42 words in one of five
   dateline forms; **quote selection** — a selected subset blockquoted, each ending at a sentence
   boundary, the remainder reported (median 2 · max 6; 0 allowed; a long quote may give its complete
   first sentence); neutral wrap-up; release boilerplate may be reused as a closing section; bylined
   pieces keep the byline (first person removed); captions stored but not displayed; categories 1–4;
   mechanical clean-up; SEO-style title.
2. **Commentary branch (class trigger, R2)** — the statement/commentary class (any org statement/
   response on a policy or decision, signed or not) published near-verbatim (reuse 95–98%). The
   **body** is reposted; the **front-matter is editor-normalised** (T-008): a clean org title is kept
   (`<Org> Press Statement/Commentary on <Topic>`), a person/reaction headline becomes `<Org>
   Commentary on <Topic>` with the person moved to the credit line; a supplied subtitle stays as a
   deck; a body dateline becomes an inset (a separate meta line is dropped); a generic opening welcome
   may be trimmed (flagged); **no** excerpt/subheaders/blockquotes; first person/org voice kept; one
   `budget-2027`-style category; credit line **only when the release supplies attribution**
   ("Kindly attribute to:" → "By"). Signed opinion/byline pieces (type table row 3) stay news-byline
   pending MSME confirm-item 2 (`ARTICLE_TYPES_AND_GATES.md`). The skill switches branches on the
   release type; deviations in either branch are `[FLAG]`ged, never silent.

rule of record. (Covered elsewhere: external facts D-002, image fallback D-003.)
