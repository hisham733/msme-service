# D-007 — What "tuned" means: the acceptance standard for handover

## Rule

The two `SKILL.md` files are **tuned** when, on the acceptance sample, the skill's output meets the
class-appropriate standard:

| class | standard |
|---|---|
| **Commentary** | **body text reuse ≥95%** of the release (already 5/5 at 95–98%) **and** the front-matter elements pass: title, credit line, dateline, categories, featured |
| **News** (corporate, data report, byline) | **≥95% of the governed element checks pass per pair** — branch, title, excerpt length + dateline form, opening, subheader count, quote selection/count, names, categories (1–4, none of PILLARS/COLUMN; slugs are editor choice), ending, featured class, 0 invented facts, traceability — **and** 0 invented facts |
| **Image skill** | **≥95% of pairs carry the correct featured class** (people / logo / event-visual / product / none), with every image traceable |

**Element = title · credit line · dateline · body · categories · featured · blockquote** (MG's list).
A published article that itself breaks a ruled rule (adds external facts, runs with no featured) is
scored as a **published deviation**, not a skill failure. The skill is judged on what it controls.

## Why — measured

A literal 95% text-similarity standard is impossible for news: the publisher rewrites news, and the
raw→published 5-gram reuse is **2–52%** for corporate news, **16–43%** for data reports and **11–67%**
for byline opinion. Only the near-verbatim commentary set reaches 95–98%. A literal standard would
fail 25 of 30 pairs by construction and would reward copying the published article — the opposite of
D-004/D-006. The element standard measures what the skill can actually get right.

## Where it is applied

- **Phase 3** of `roadmap/roadmap.md`: the 22 not-yet-diffed corpus pairs, then the cold run on 2 new
  MSME releases.
- A new release has no published copy yet; it is scored on **rule conformance + MG's review** at the
  cold run, and diffed against the published copy when MSME publishes it (post-handover check).

**Rollback.** One line: restore a literal text-similarity threshold (not recommended; it fails news
by construction).
