# D-023 — The measuring instrument is not the configuration

## Rule

1. The **Filter engine owner** decides the gate weights, the accept cut, and where those values are
   stored. Nothing in this repository's export writes or prescribes them.
2. The exported `filter_stage/` is a **read-only measurement kit**: `parse` · `report --cut N` · `fit` ·
   `selftest`, plus the measured documents. It ships no client workbooks, no dataset, and no config file
   for the partner's engine.
3. Defaults in the kit (`CUT = 62`, `W_SHEET`, `W_MEASURED`) exist to **reproduce the 2026-10
   measurement**, not to set a policy. The kit's `--cut` exists so the owner can measure their own value.
4. The partner's LLM chooses integrate / defer / ignore (`FILTER_KIT.md` §5) and records it in
   `INTEGRATION_REPORT.md`.

## Why

The export's job is to hand over the repurpose → `.html` chain. The filter sits upstream, in a pipeline
we do not run, with a configuration we cannot see. A fixed tunable in our artifact would (a) be wrong
after the next batch and (b) blur who owns the decision. The measured operating point is still recorded —
as a measurement with its date and denominators, which is the honest form.

**Rollback.** Remove `filter_stage/` from the export and the FILTER_KIT pointer; the rest is unaffected.
