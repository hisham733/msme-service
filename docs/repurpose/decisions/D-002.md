# D-002 — External-facts policy = C (never insert; flag opportunities)

## Rule

The repurpose pipeline **never inserts facts outside the press release**, even where the live site's
older articles do. Where the article would clearly benefit, it emits a **context opportunity** for the
editor: *what to add, from which source class, where it would go* — selected per the rules in
`analysis/repurpose/EXTERNAL_FACTS_PLAYBOOK.md`. Insertion only ever happens with an MSME-approved
context source configured into the pipeline.

## Why — measured

Docx §5 forbids external facts; over the 30-pair corpus the publisher added **20 facts in 10/30
articles** (B1: 9/17 articles, 18 facts; B2: 1/13, 2 facts; max 6 — Mercedes specs/prices;
typical additions = the missing policy anchor: Cabinet date, RM1,700 minimum wage, trade figures).
The playbook distils when the publisher adds context and what a "good" opportunity looks like;
without this decision the skill cannot (a) finalise Step 9 or (b) let the pilot run.

**Rollback.** One line in the skill's Step 9 switches it back to docx-strict (no flags).
