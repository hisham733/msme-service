KUALA LUMPUR, 12 NOVEMBER 2026 — As Malaysian SMEs race to digitise, one local fintech says the real bottleneck isn't technology but cash-flow timing.

According to its latest SME survey, 7 in 10 small businesses say late payments — not sales — are their biggest threat. The company says its new cash-flow platform helps SMEs predict gaps and smooth expenses 30 days ahead.

"Most SMEs don't fail because they can't sell; they fail because money arrives late," said Amira Tan, Head of Growth. "We built a platform that gives founders visibility before a crunch."

The platform, launched this month, connects to common accounting tools and flags upcoming shortfalls. "It changed how we plan," said one SME owner who adopted it early.

The tool is free to try, with paid plans from RM199/month, and the company is offering 20% off annual subscriptions until 31 December. "We want prudent cash-flow habits to become the norm," the spokesperson added.

Analysts say cash-flow management is a growing priority as B2B payment terms stretch. Whether tools like this change behaviour at scale remains to be seen — but for SMEs that dread the mid-month squeeze, the appeal is obvious.

SMEs can learn more and sign up on the company's website.
