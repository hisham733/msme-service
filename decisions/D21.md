# D21 — Attachment logos count

## Rule

Every standalone image attachment is classified with `extract._kind_from_bytes` (now including a wide
banner rule: `max/min ≥ 3.0` and `max ≥ 500` → **logo**). A logo with max dim ≥ 200 → `hasLogo=true` and is
saved as `images/attachmentlogo-N.png`; small icons/signatures are skipped. Photos still require the
deferred `D14` dimension rule to become `hasPic`.

## Why — measured

Jobstreet by SEEK `1a143640d45b515d`: the email has the full commentary in the body (subject 95) and one
inline attachment `inline_image` (PNG, 33,569 B, **1033×275**) — visually the **Jobstreet by SEEK logo**.
The old code only checked attachment *filenames* for "logo" and required 1 MB for `hasPic`, so it was
ignored. After the fix: `hasLogo=true`, `readyToFilter=true`; live run `fb5cd9a2-…`, row 367.

**Rollback.** Remove the attachment-logo classification and the wide-banner rule.
