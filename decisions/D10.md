# D10 — Subject match: headline + first paragraph

## Rule

Compare the email subject with three things: the **headline**, the **first paragraph after the headline**,
and the **chosen attachment filename**. Two signals count:

1. **Exact label** — the normalised subject is a contiguous phrase in the headline, first paragraph or
   filename.
2. **Key-token coverage** — at least **60 %** of the subject's key tokens (≥ 2 tokens) appear in the
   headline + first paragraph. Key tokens are words ≥ 3 chars after removing generic label words
   (`press, statement, release, news, media, advisory, update, commentary, fwd, re…`) and stopwords.

Then `subjectMatch=true`; `subjectScore = max(AI score, 85)` for an exact label, or
`max(AI score, 70 + 25 × coverage)` capped at 95 for coverage. Record the basis in the verify stage.
Keyword overlap on one generic word alone is not enough.

## Why — measured

ACCCIM `1a120993ce682d83`: subject is a label, headline is different, but the label is line 1 of the PDF
and in the filename. Of the 13 current `subjectMatch=false` rows, coverage ≥ 0.60 flags **5**, and all 5
were inspected and are the correct article: ACCCIM (1.00), OCBC (0.83), PIKOM (0.80), redBus (1.00),
Mimecast (0.64). An unrelated subject/article pair scored ~0.

**Rollback.** Remove the deterministic override and the prompt paragraph; AI-only comparison returns.
