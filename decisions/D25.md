# D25 — Document-processing caps

## Rule

1. **Images:** at most **10 images per PDF/DOCX** are classified and saved. To find
   those 10, at most **25** images are examined (`DOC_IMAGE_SCAN_MAX`). No file is
   exhausted.
2. **Text:** a PDF is read only from its **first 30 pages**, and no document yields
   more than **150,000 characters**. The text ends with a visible truncation
   marker so the extract AI knows it is partial.
3. **No 10 MB hard ignore.** Large files are processed with the caps above; a
   38 MB report or a 21 MB DOCX is still usable. The earlier 10 MB ignore idea is
   dropped because it would have removed Meltwater's only media and Workplace's
   article.
4. Applies to every document source: Gmail attachments, Drive files/folders,
   OneDrive, Box and Google-native PDF exports. Images (standalone photos/logos)
   are unaffected.

## Why — measured

`[MEDIA] Digital 2027 Global Report.pdf` is **38.7 MB, 906 pages**. Extracting all
text took **>240 s** (the full run took ~15 minutes). Image work was only ~46 s.

After the caps:

| Step | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Text extraction | all 906 pages (>240 s) | 30 pages, 29,564 chars, **25.4 s** |
| Image classification | 25 images, 15.6 s | 10 images, **8.2 s** |
| Photo extraction | all images, 29.7 s | up to 25 examined / 10 saved, **27.5 s** |
| End-to-end Meltwater (fake AI, real Gmail/Drive) | ~15 min | **114.9 s** |

Regression sample (fake AI, real sources): Workplace **47.6 s** (ready, 5 images
archived), Pink October **150 s** (ready, 190 MB extra PDF capped), MRCA **7.7 s**
(ready). All four keep `readyToFilter=true`.

**Live AI re-run was blocked by DeepSeek HTTP 402 (insufficient balance)**, after
the document stage completed with `textLen=29,564`; see `T-038`. The previous
Meltwater row **378** remains ready.

## Rollback

Remove the three limits (`MAX_DOC_IMAGES`, `PDF_TEXT_MAX_PAGES`,
`DOC_TEXT_MAX_CHARS`) and the caps in `extract.py`.
