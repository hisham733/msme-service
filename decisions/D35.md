# D35 — Repurpose integration

> **Superseded by `D40` (2026-11-06):** the engine is vendored under `msme/repurpose/engine/`
> and runs in-process on :8899. This file is the historical D35 record.

## Rule

1. **The engine is external and untouched**: `/home/hisham/Desktop/new_div/MSME_AI_News`.
   We do not fork or modify its code.
2. Our adapter is `repurpose_export.py`: after an **ACCEPT** is saved, when
   `ENABLE_AUTO_REPURPOSE=true`, it copies the latest accepted article (one release `.md`, `images/`,
   `originals/`) to `repurpose_inbox/<subject>/`. Existing engine work is never overwritten.
3. The engine reads that inbox (`config.local.json`) and writes the built site to
   `msme-service/repurpose_site/`.
4. `repurpose_worker.py` watches the inbox and runs the engine **one article at a time**,
   automatically, skipping folders with `draft.md`/`run.json`. The engine dashboard at
   **http://localhost:8765/** remains the operator surface for manual Run/Validate and HOLD rules.
5. The engine uses the current DeepSeek key stored in its gitignored `.env`; no key is committed or
   shown in a reply.
6. `repurpose_inbox/` and `repurpose_site/` are gitignored client/output data.
