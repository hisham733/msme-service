# D12 — Release-named doc wins over the longest doc

## Rule

When a Drive media kit has several documents, choose the article by **name semantics**, not by text
length:

- **Release names** (strong): `siaran akhbar`, `siaran media`, `kenyataan media/akhbar`, `press release`,
  `news release`, `media release`, `media statement`, `statement`, `nota kepada editor`,
  `hebahan serta-merta`, `for immediate release`.
- **Speech names** (exclude/deprioritise): `ucapan`, `teks ucapan`, `speech`, `remarks`, `keynote`,
  `welcoming`, `opening address`, `perasmian`.
- Tie-break: English name, then longest text.

The same picker must feed both the extract prompt (`format_media_kit`) and the `attachment_text` fallback.
A release-named document is never displaced by a longer speech.

## Why — measured

HARI EKSPORT PERAK `1a13f5a7dce62d0a`: the Drive folder held the release DOCX
`[SIARAN AKHBAR] HARI EKSPORT PERAK 2026…docx` (6,150 chars, headline **exactly** the email subject),
plus `Ucapan Aluan …pdf` (11,022 chars) and `TEKS UCAPAN PERASMIAN …pdf` (10,350). The pipeline picked
the **longest** doc (welcome speech) for `attachment_text`; verify correctly said "TEKS UCAPAN ALUAN",
`hasArticle=false`, subject score 10 — the release was present but never selected.

**Rollback.** Restore the longest-text selection; the speech wins again.
