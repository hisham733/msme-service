# D11 — Repeated wide strips are banners/logos

## Rule

In `classify_doc_images`, a raster image that appears on **more than one page** and is **wide** (aspect
ratio `max/min ≥ 3.0`) is a header/footer **banner or logo**, never an article photo — even when it is
large (the old rule only covered repeated images ≤ 700 px). It sets `docLogo`, not `docPhoto`.

## Why — measured

Email `1a120993ce682d83` (ACCCIM Budget 2027) PDF has two images repeated on all 3 pages:
`/Image9` 1653×399 (aspect 4.14) and `/Image11` 1653×333 (4.96). The rendered page shows the first is the
ACCCIM logo/letterhead strip and the second the red footer band. Both were classified `photo`, so the
checklist said `hasImageInArticle=true`. Repeated page-wide strips are branding; a news photo does not
repeat on every page.

**Rollback.** Revert the aspect-ratio clause; wide repeated strips become photos again.
