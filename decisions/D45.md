# D45 — Repurpose image/link discovery

## Rule

The received email is the root; two child tables carry what repurpose needs:

- **`email_links`** — the report/speech/source link per email (`link_type`, `url`, `source`,
  `anchor`, `confidence`, `is_primary`, `candidates_json`), keyed by `message_id`.
- **`email_media`** — one row per collected image/logo (`name`, `kind`, `source`,
  `role`, `path`, `width`, `height`, `bytes`, `sha1`, `usable`, `chosen`), keyed by `message_id`.
  `source='gallery'` + `chosen=1` records the N-1 gallery fallback.

**Architecture:** the vendored engine is file-based, so the tables are the source of truth and the
**export materialises** them — it writes `meta.json` (reportUrl/speechUrl) into
`repurpose_inbox/<folder>/`; the engine appends the line **at build time** (after validation).

## N-3 (built) — report link

- Detector (`report_link.py`): DOCX `document.xml.rels` hyperlinks (Type=hyperlink) + PDF
  `/URI` annotations + email body links; scored by path/anchor (report/insights/trends/digital-20xx/
  download; social/publisher/unsubscribe excluded); a hit needs a report phrase **and** a scored link.
- `emailType=report`; `email_links(link_type='report')`.
- Engine appends **"Read the full report: <url>"** at the end of the body at build.

## N-4 (capture-only) — speech link

- Detector (`gov_summary.py`): government/budget markers + a speech-like link → `email_links(link_type='speech')`,
  `emailType=government_summary`.
- **Capture-only:** never fetches the speech and never turns a no-article email into a published one.
  The summary-vs-speech threshold is MSME decision B (open); the fetch and the article override wait
  for it.

## Why

The validator `NO_CONTACTS_BOILERPLATE` fails a draft whose body contains a URL, so the link cannot
live in `draft.md`/`article.md`; it must be injected after validation. Putting the link and media in
SQLite gives the editor and repurpose one queryable store, while a small `meta.json` bridges to the
file-based engine.

## Rollback

Remove the `report`/`government` stage in `pipeline.py` and the `email_links`/`email_media` tables;
the engine ignores a missing `meta.json`. N-4 capture is inert without the pipeline stage.
