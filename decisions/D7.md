# D7 — Body-embedded images count as hasPic

## Rule

The receive reads remote `<img src="http(s)…">` URLs from the HTML body and downloads them. A body
image counts as a picture when **dimensions** classify it as a photo (`extract._kind_from_bytes`:
max dim ≥ 500 px, min dim > 150 px), floor 10 KB, cap 8 MB, max 12 URLs — the 1 MB attachment rule does
**not** apply to web-sized photos. Kept images are saved to the article's `images/`, passed to verify,
and make `hasPic=true`.

`hasImageInArticle` keeps its old meaning: an image **inside** an attached PDF/DOCX, not an inline
body photo. A generic web page linked in the email (e.g. vritimes) is still **not** fetched — `T-013`.

## Why — measured

Email `1a109711b5288f0f` (IRGCE 2026) had no attachments, 5 inline images on `imagedelivery.net`
(640×427 JPEG, 29–59 KB) and reported `hasPic=false`, `readyToFilter=false`. The old pipeline only read
`<a href>` links and only counted downloaded files ≥ 1 MB. A test fetch returned `image/jpeg` 200 and
`_kind_from_bytes` said `photo`.

**Rollback.** Remove the `body_images` stage; body photos stop counting.
