# D41 — Webhook runtime switch

## Rule

1. `ENABLE_WEBHOOK` and `GMAIL_PUBSUB_TOPIC` are saved settings, editable at runtime; the env
   values are only defaults.
2. The watch is created/renewed only when **`ENABLE_WEBHOOK=true` AND a topic is set**. The old
   OR behaviour is superseded: OFF must really mean off.
3. The Config → Receive card (`GET/POST /api/webhook`) is the control. Turning it on calls
   `users.watch` immediately; turning it off calls `users.stop` and clears the stored cursor.
4. While OFF, `POST /webhooks/gmail` returns **200 `{"status":"disabled"}`** without touching
   Gmail, so Pub/Sub does not retry and no email is processed.
5. If the voice is disabled, polling (`ENABLE_AUTO=true`) is the fallback; the card warns when
   both are off.
6. `/api/webhook` is admin-only; `/api/health` reports the live flag.

## Why

The webhook was env-only (`ENABLE_WEBHOOK or GMAIL_PUBSUB_TOPIC`): an operator could not switch it
off without editing `docker-compose.yml`, recreating the container, and stopping the Gmail watch by
hand. The OR condition also meant clearing the flag still renewed the watch. The switch makes the
state visible and immediate.

## Rollback

Set `ENABLE_WEBHOOK=false` in `docker-compose.yml` and remove the topic; the env default applies.
