## T-006 Decide the main.py / dashboard.py route duplication

`main.py` (:8898) and `dashboard.py` (:8899) expose the same operations under two prefixes
(`/run` vs `/api/run`, `/config` vs `/api/config`, …), each with its own copy of `_process_one` and
the config patch model. Any behaviour fix must be made twice today. Decide: one shared router imported
by both, or retire one surface.

**Done when.** The user picks one; the change names the curl/steps proving both ports still answer.
