## ✅ T-015 Report runner should read both archive roots

✅ **DONE 2026-10-23 — `D26`.**

`/app/all_emails` in Docker) through `archive_layout.py`:

  newest folder with an article `.md`);
- `_duplicates/` and `_no_article/` are excluded;

  `versions` sheet.

`since=2026-10-05` range · 0 duplicate versions**; `/archived` shows **140 ready
rows, all with an openable article** (the one Malay duplicate row was marked
not-ready). Tests: `python3 -m unittest tests.test_report_versions -v` → 10
pass; `tests.test_collect_select` → 15 pass.
