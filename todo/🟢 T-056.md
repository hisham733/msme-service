## 🟢 T-056 Repurpose image/link discovery (N-3/N-4)

**Goal:** a formal three-table model under the received email so the repurpose stage can find (a) the
image/logo — including the N-1 gallery fallback — and (b) the report/speech link, without guessing.

**Architecture constraint:** the vendored engine is **file-based** (reads `repurpose_inbox/<folder>/`
with `article.md`, `images/`, `originals/`, `meta.json`); it never reads SQLite. The tables are the
source of truth; the export **materialises** them into the inbox folder. The engine is unchanged.

### Schema (additive; no FK constraints — links by `message_id`, matching the existing file)

- **`emails`** (exists, `db.save_email` @ `pipeline.py:480`) — the receive root.
- **`email_links`** (new): `message_id`, `checklist_id`, `link_type` (report·speech·source·other),
  `url`, `source` (docx_rels·pdf_uri·email_body·web), `anchor`, `confidence`, `is_primary`,
  `candidates_json`, `created_at`.
- **`email_media`** (new): `message_id`, `checklist_id`, `name`, `kind` (photo·logo·unknown),
  `source` (email_attachment·doc_embed·body·drive·web·**gallery**·library_update),
  `role` (featured·logo·candidate), `path`, `width`, `height`, `bytes`, `sha1`,
  `usable` (vector or max dim ≥ `BODY_LOGO_MIN_DIM`), `chosen` (receive intent), `created_at`.

### Notes / risks

- Engine `imgselect` picks the **featured** image; `email_media.chosen` records receive's *intent*
  (gallery logo when N-1 fired), not the final pick — the engine stays authoritative.
- The report/speech link is appended at build, never in `draft.md`/`article.md` (validator).
- `emails.links_json` stays alongside `email_links` (sender/cost pages still read it).
- Detector is conservative: a hit needs a report phrase **and** a scored report-like URL; a miss is a
  normal article.
