## ✅ T-051 AI Model → Cost page

**Final shape (2026-11-07, after the user's follow-up):** `static/by-email.html` →
`static/ai-model.html` is the **Cost** page.

- Spend cards (total, chain, avg/email, avg/published), stage split, budget bar, 14-day
  sparkline and most-expensive email.

  remain aliases. The editor page no longer links to it.
