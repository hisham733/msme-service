## ✅ T-053 Webhook on/off

**What changed:**

- `ENABLE_WEBHOOK` and `GMAIL_PUBSUB_TOPIC` are now runtime settings.
- `GET /api/webhook` shows the live flag, topic, stored watch expiry and whether polling is on.
- `POST /api/webhook {enabled}` saves the flag and immediately calls Gmail `users.watch` /
  `users.stop`; a wrong token is reported in the card, not silently swallowed.
- The public `/webhooks/gmail` route answers **200 disabled** (Pub/Sub does not retry) and makes
  no Gmail call while the flag is off.
- The start condition is now **`ENABLE_WEBHOOK` AND topic** (recorded in `D41`).
- Config → Receive card warns when turning the webhook off while `ENABLE_AUTO=false` (no intake).

**Proof:** `tests/test_webhook.py` 28 tests (4 new toggle tests) · `/api/health` reports
`"webhook": true` live · container rebuilt and healthy.
