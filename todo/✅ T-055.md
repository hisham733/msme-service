## ✅ T-055 Cost of a receive → publish chain

**What changed:**

- `msme/cost.py` estimates USD from stored tokens: DeepSeek **$0.65/1M total**, Jev
  **$0.042/1M input** (defaults are the measured rates; editable).
- The repurpose engine now records usage on every chat/vision call
  (`engine/usage.py`); `repurpose_runs.usage_json` and `run.json` carry it.
- The AI Model page has a **Cost** tab (admin only): total spend (all runs), chain spend
  (latest per email), avg/email, avg/published, stage split, budget bar with “≈ N emails left”,
  14-day sparkline, most expensive email, token totals and a per-email cost table.
- `GET /api/costs.json` returns `{summary, rows, pricing}`.
- Config → Cost edits the four rates/budget.

**Measured on the live DB (2026-11-07):** all runs **≈ $2.06** (250 receive runs + 24 Jev
results) · chain (168 emails) **≈ $1.40** · avg/email **≈ $0.00833** · most expensive
Meltwater `1a162cc0b30af0e7` **≈ $0.0319** · repurpose **not measured yet** (0 runs).

**Proof:** `tests/test_cost.py` + `tests/test_repurpose_usage.py` · full suite 241 tests ·
`/api/costs.json` = 401 without a session · container rebuilt.
