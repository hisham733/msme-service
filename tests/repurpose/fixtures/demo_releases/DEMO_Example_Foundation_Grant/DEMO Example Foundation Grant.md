# Example Foundation Announces Coastal Cleanup Grant

GEORGE TOWN, 3 FEBRUARY 2026 — Example Foundation, a fictional grantmaker used only for pipeline
testing, announced a RM2.4 million grant for coastal cleanup groups in Penang. The grant will fund 15
organisations over two years and is not a real programme.

Each selected group will receive up to RM160,000 for equipment, training and monitoring. The foundation
said applications open on 1 March 2026 and close on 30 April 2026. Decisions will be announced in June.

"Small groups do the daily work of keeping a coastline clean, and they often do it with volunteers and
borrowed equipment," said Daniel Lim, programme director at Example Foundation. "This grant is meant to
make that work less fragile."

The foundation said the grant will prioritise groups that can show three years of cleanup records and a
monitoring plan. It will publish the full criteria on its fictional website and hold two briefing
sessions in Penang.

About Example Foundation

Example Foundation is a fictional organisation created for this handoff. It supports community
