# Example Robotics Opens Demo Innovation Centre in Cyberjaya

CYBERJAYA, 15 JANUARY 2026 — Example Robotics Sdn Bhd, a fictional automation company used only to
test the AI_News pipeline, today opened a demonstration centre in Cyberjaya. The centre is a test bed,
not a real facility, and every figure in this document is invented for the purpose of exercising the
repurpose and image stages.

The company said the centre houses 12 collaborative robot cells and a small training room for 40
people. It expects to train 300 technicians in its first year and to run 24 demonstration sessions a
month for manufacturers that are evaluating automation.

"This centre exists so a factory owner can see a robot cell working before committing to a purchase,"
said Aina Rahman, chief executive of Example Robotics. "A brochure cannot show cycle time. A live cell
can."

Example Robotics said the fit-out cost RM8.5 million and was supported by a fictional state agency.
The centre will employ 18 people, including 6 service engineers. It plans a second centre in Johor in
2027, subject to demand.

The company also announced a partnership with Example Technical College to place 30 students a year in
paid internships. The first intake begins in March 2026.

About Example Robotics

Example Robotics is a fictional company created for this handoff. It designs and installs automation
