## Title

BMW Motorrad Malaysia Adds Five-Year Service and Warranty Cover, Financing From 2.99%

## Excerpt

KUALA LUMPUR, 16 OCTOBER 2026 - Riders can now finance selected BMW Motorrad models with five years of service and warranty coverage at rates from 2.99% to 3.30% over a tenure of up to seven years.

## Body

BMW Motorrad Malaysia has introduced its enhanced BMW Motorrad Service Inclusive (BMSI) and BMW Motorrad Warranty Extension (BMWE) offerings, extending its ownership proposition beyond the point of purchase. Selected models can be financed together with both programmes at rates from 2.99% to 3.30% over a tenure of up to seven years through BMW Group Financial Services Malaysia.

The company said the initiative reflects a customer-centric approach in which protection, maintenance and long-term ownership confidence are integrated into the riding experience, so that premium care is easier to plan for.

Riders may take up either programme on its own, or both together as a combo package.

## What the Programmes Cover

BMSI covers scheduled maintenance, including genuine parts, labour and oil, for five years or 50,000 km, whichever comes first.

BMWE adds two years to the standard three-year warranty, for five years of warranty coverage in total.

Pricing varies by model, starting from RM3,100 for BMSI, RM1,000 for BMWE or RM4,100 for both. The full price list by model is available on the BMW Motorrad Malaysia website.

## Financing From 2.99%

Through BMW Group Financial Services Malaysia, both programmes are included in the financing of selected models at rates from 2.99% to 3.30%, compared with 4.60% under standard financing without the programmes, over a tenure of up to seven years.

A single monthly instalment then covers the motorcycle, its servicing and its extended warranty, so riders know what ownership will cost in advance. Terms and conditions apply.

## Cover That Stays With the Motorcycle

Both programmes are attached to the motorcycle and remain valid with subsequent owners, which BMW Motorrad Malaysia said supports its resale value when a rider decides to upgrade to another BMW Motorrad.

The arrangement is designed to keep riders flexible over the length of their ownership, with the choice of either programme alone or both as a package.

## Manufacturer-Backed, Dealer-Delivered

Unlike conventional aftermarket solutions, the programmes are backed by the manufacturer and delivered through the authorised BMW Motorrad dealer network worldwide. Maintenance and repairs are carried out using BMW Motorrad Genuine Parts and in accordance with BMW Motorrad's recommended standards.

The company said this ensures quality, reliability and consistency throughout the ownership lifecycle.

Feedback from customers has highlighted the value of knowing their motorcycles are cared for by trained BMW Motorrad specialists, using genuine components and approved processes, which for many riders translates into greater confidence, predictable ownership costs and assurance that their motorcycle continues to perform as intended over time.

## Beyond the Showroom

> "At BMW Motorrad, our commitment to customers extends far beyond the motorcycle itself."

Tan said the company continuously sought ways to enhance the ownership experience by offering greater peace of mind, and care, convenience and confidence throughout every stage of the rider's journey.

BMW Motorrad Malaysia described its approach to ownership as holistic, extending past engineering and products to support riders long after they leave the showroom, through an authorised nationwide dealer network, dedicated aftersales expertise and customer care solutions.

As the riding community continues to grow, the company said it remained committed to delivering solutions that provide greater convenience, transparency and confidence, backed by the same passion, quality and innovation that define the BMW Motorrad brand. The enhanced BMSI and BMWE programmes are available through authorised BMW Motorrad dealers, with the full price list by model published on the BMW Motorrad Malaysia website.

## About BMW Group Malaysia

BMW Group Malaysia is the representative of Bayerische Motoren Werke (BMW) AG in Malaysia, with activities covering the wholesale of BMW, MINI and BMW Motorrad vehicles, spare parts and accessories, and the planning of sales, marketing, after-sales and related activities in Malaysia. Its presence includes the National Sales Centre in Cyberjaya and BMW Assembly Facilities at the Kulim Hi-Tech Park in Kedah, together with the BMW Group Data Centre, the BMW Group Regional Training Centre in Cyberjaya and the BMW Group Parts Distribution Centre at the Free Industrial Zone of the Senai International Airport in Johor, which supports 22 markets in the Asia-Pacific region. The BMW dealership network covers 39 outlets in various cities in Malaysia.
